# Password Policy Guidance (Legacy, 2012)

This legacy guidance recommends the following password policy:

- Require a minimum length of 8 characters.
- Require complexity: at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one
  digit, and one special character.
- Force users to change their passwords every 90 days.
- Prevent reuse of the last 5 passwords.

Mandatory periodic rotation every 90 days is considered a core control in this
older guidance.
